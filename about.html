ABOUT SIXTH GEAR DIGITAL

Clear marketing for industrial businesses.

I’m Alex, founder of Sixth Gear Digital. I help industrial businesses explain their work online, reach customers through search and keep track of the requests that come in.

I work on ads, website content and page design. We start with what you do, who you want to reach and what your team needs to know before it can help a customer.

Talk about your business ↗

WHO WE WORK WITH

Businesses with work to explain.

Sixth Gear works with manufacturers, equipment suppliers, repair companies and specialist contractors. Their customers may need a repair today, a piece of equipment next week or a quote for a larger project.

Each need calls for different information. Our industry pages show examples of the questions buyers ask and the details your team may need.

We agree on where ads will run based on the areas you serve and where you can deliver. We discuss that before starting the work.

WHAT WE DO

Help people find you and get in touch.

Help customers find your business

Google Ads can reach people searching for your services. Search engine optimization, or SEO, helps people find your website through search.

Make it easy to take the next step

Websites and landing pages explain your work and help buyers ask for a quote. Lead tracking and follow-up help your team keep track of requests and reply at the right time.

HOW WE WORK

Agree on the work. Keep it clear.

Understand your business

We ask about your services, products and customers. We also need to know which jobs you want and which ones are not a fit.

Make a clear plan

We agree on the pages, ads and other work, along with costs and who does what. Ad spending and extra software costs are discussed separately.

Review what happens

We look at the calls and requests your team receives. Where records are available, we also check which ones become quotes and jobs.
