ABOUT SIXTH GEAR DIGITAL

Industrial marketing with a clear commercial purpose.

I’m Alex, founder of Sixth Gear Digital. I connect search campaigns, website experiences and sales follow-up so industrial businesses can turn interest into useful conversations.

My work brings together paid advertising, content and website design. The starting point is your business: the capabilities you want to sell, the buyers you need to reach and the information your sales team needs to qualify an opportunity.

Talk about your business ↗

WHO THE WORK IS FOR

Companies with technical capabilities to explain.

Sixth Gear focuses on manufacturers, equipment suppliers, industrial service teams and specialist contractors. Their buyers may be arranging a repair today, comparing equipment for a quote or evaluating a supplier for a longer project.

Those journeys need different pages and different qualification criteria. Explore the industry marketing plans for examples of the buyer questions, capability information and sales handoffs we would consider.

Campaign coverage is agreed around your actual service territory, delivery capability and target markets. We establish the geographic scope during discovery rather than advertising every location by default.

HOW THE WORK FITS TOGETHER

From the first search to the next sales action.

Find relevant demand

Industrial Google Ads targets commercial searches aligned with your capabilities and coverage. Industrial SEO builds useful service, equipment and application content for buyers evaluating suppliers.

Make the enquiry useful

Websites and landing pages explain fit and capture the details sales needs. Lead tracking and follow-up connect that enquiry with an owner, a next action and a recorded outcome.

A PRACTICAL WORKING PROCESS

Agree on the work and how to judge it.

Start with capability and fit

Review the services, equipment, applications and territories you actually support. Define which enquiries are worthwhile and which should be excluded.

Make the scope visible

Agree on pages, campaigns, tracking, required access and who approves technical claims. Advertising spend and outside tools are discussed separately from the delivery scope.

Review sales feedback

Use qualified enquiries, quote progress and recorded outcomes to assess the work. A form submission starts the conversation; sales feedback tells us whether the targeting fits.

The dispatch, equipment and project intake examples show how those decisions change a page. They are planning examples, not reported client results.

START A CONVERSATION

Tell us what your business wants to grow.

Use the growth-plan form to share your company, operating territory and highest-value capability. That gives us a starting point for discussing fit, priorities and a practical scope.
