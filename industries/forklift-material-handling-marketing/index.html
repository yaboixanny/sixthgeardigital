A plan for your equipment and services

We start with the forklifts you sell, the rental options you offer and the service work you want. New equipment, used trucks and breakdown support need different messages. Your territory, stock and workshop capacity guide the plan.

Ads that separate sales, hire and repairs

We build Google Ads around what the buyer needs, from forklift hire to a replacement truck or local service support. Each campaign can use the right area and page, helping buyers reach the part of your business that can help.

Equipment pages with useful specifications

We help organize capacity, lift height, power type, condition and application details. Clear listings and service pages help warehouse teams compare options. Your staff checks equipment information so the pages reflect what you can supply and support.

Can you separate forklift sales, rentals and service inquiries?

Yes. Those buyers have different questions and timelines. We plan the pages, campaigns and forms around each offer so sales and service teams can receive the information relevant to their work.

What should a forklift quote request ask for?

Start with the job, location, required date and whether the customer wants to buy or rent. Equipment type, load requirements and working environment can help your team assess fit. Your equipment specialists should confirm the specification before recommending a model.

Do product pages need to show every available forklift?

Not always. Clear equipment categories and useful specifications can be a practical starting point. Where you publish individual machines, keep availability and details current and explain how buyers can check the latest options.

How do we know which inquiries become revenue?

Connect calls and forms to their source where possible, then record whether they concern sales, rental or service. Review qualified requests, quotes, rental bookings and orders separately so one busy line does not hide another.

What does material handling marketing cost?

It depends on the equipment range, coverage, inventory content and tracking work. We agree on tasks and fees first. Ad spending and any extra software costs are discussed separately.
