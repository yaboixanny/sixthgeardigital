05 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before we get started.

Do we need a 3D configurator to generate inquiries?

No. A clear project brief can be a strong starting point. A configurator is a separate feature that needs approved options, constraints, and a defined role in your estimating process.

Can campaigns distinguish manufacturers from erectors?

Yes. We make the offered scope explicit in ad copy, landing pages, and form options so buyers can understand whether your business fits the project.

Should the website show instant building prices?

Only when you can maintain approved pricing rules and explain their limits. Otherwise, collecting the core project details for a scoped estimate is more reliable than an unsupported instant price.
