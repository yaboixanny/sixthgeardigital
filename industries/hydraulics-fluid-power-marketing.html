05 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before we get started.

Can campaigns run only during our staffed hours?

Yes. We can scope ad schedules around your availability and make after-hours arrangements clear. A 24/7 message should only appear if your business provides that service.

Can we promote rebuild work alongside emergency repairs?

Yes. Planned rebuilds and emergency calls should have separate messaging, landing pages, and inquiry routes so each reaches the appropriate team.

How would missed-call follow-up work?

We first review your phone system, dispatch process, and messaging permissions. Any follow-up automation is scoped around those systems rather than assumed to be available.
