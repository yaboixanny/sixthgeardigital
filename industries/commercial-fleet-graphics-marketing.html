05 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before we get started.

Can campaigns focus on fleet projects instead of personal cars?

Yes. We use commercial language, appropriate search targeting, exclusions, and form questions to establish fleet fit. Inquiry quality still needs ongoing review.

What project content should we supply?

Approved photos, vehicle types, installation locations, and a factual description of the rollout are useful. We avoid publishing client names or results without permission.

Can you help follow up on open quotes?

We can scope CRM tasks and email follow-up around your sales process. The aim is to make the next step clear while respecting the prospect’s communication preferences.
