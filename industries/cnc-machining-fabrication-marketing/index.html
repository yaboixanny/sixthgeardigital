What can you expect working with us as a CNC machining and fabrication marketing agency?

A plan for the parts you want to make

We start with your machines, materials, tolerances and preferred order sizes. Prototype work, production runs and fabrication projects need different messages. The plan focuses on the capabilities and capacity your shop wants to promote.

Ads for relevant machining work

We build Google Ads around searches for machining services, fabrication processes and the materials you work with. We filter out job searches, courses and machine sales where possible, helping your budget reach buyers looking for a supplier.

Capability pages engineers can assess

We help present your processes, equipment and material capabilities clearly. Certification and tolerance claims stay grounded in what your team confirms. Engineers and purchasing staff can find the information they need to decide whether to send an RFQ.

RFQs with the details sales needs

We improve quote forms to ask about material, quantity, process and delivery needs. Where file uploads are needed, we agree on a suitable way to receive them. Your team gets a clearer starting point for reviewing the work.

Follow-up through supplier selection

We help keep RFQs, quotes and buyer conversations organized in your customer records. Follow-up steps can reflect sample approval, supplier reviews and production schedules, so a longer buying process is easier to track.

Reporting on suitable quote requests

We review which searches and pages bring work that fits your shop. Your team’s feedback on RFQs, quotes and won jobs helps us improve the marketing. You can see the work planned and how the next decisions are made.

Can you help us attract the machining work we actually want?

We plan around the processes, materials, quantities and customers that fit your shop. Clear capability pages and focused campaigns can help buyers assess whether you handle prototypes, production runs, fabrication or specialist work.

What should buyers see before sending a machining RFQ?

Show approved information about materials, equipment, part sizes, tolerances and certifications. Useful project examples can help explain fit. A first RFQ should capture the process or part, quantity and required date, with a suitable way to share drawings arranged by your team.

Should a machine shop start with Google Ads or SEO?

Google Ads can test demand for a specific process, material or application while SEO builds useful capability pages over time. If your website does not explain what you can handle or make an RFQ easy, address those gaps first.

How do we measure marketing through a long sales cycle?

Track the source, fit and quote status of each request where your tools allow. Keep open opportunities as well as won and lost orders. Supplier reviews and technical discussions can take time, so a form submission alone does not tell the full story.

What does CNC machining marketing cost?

It depends on your target work, markets, website content and tracking needs. We agree on tasks and fees before starting. Google Ads spending and any extra software costs are discussed separately.
