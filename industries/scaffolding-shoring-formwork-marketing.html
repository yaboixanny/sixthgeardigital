05 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before we get started.

Can you target the areas our branches serve?

Yes. We align local content and campaign coverage with real branches and service territories. Location targeting is reviewed alongside actual inquiry quality.

Can leads be routed to the right rental representative?

Routing can be scoped around branch ownership, product lines, and your CRM. The project intake should capture the information used to make that assignment.

Does an online quote request cover the engineering review?

No. The form starts a commercial discussion. Equipment selection, design, installation, and project-specific engineering remain part of your qualified team’s assessment.
