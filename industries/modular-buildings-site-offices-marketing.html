05 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before we get started.

Can marketing distinguish rentals from building sales?

Yes. We can create separate campaigns, page sections, and inquiry questions for rental and purchase opportunities, including lease duration where relevant.

How do you describe blast-resistant modules?

We use your approved technical documentation and product descriptions. Marketing should direct specification questions to qualified staff and avoid implying unsupported certifications or performance ratings.

Can inquiries be assigned to regional lease managers?

Routing can be scoped around your territories and CRM. We confirm ownership rules and the information each regional team needs before setting it up.
