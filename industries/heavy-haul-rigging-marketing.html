05 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before we get started.

Can campaigns focus on particular transport corridors?

Yes. Geographic targeting and landing-page content can reflect your actual route coverage and preferred project areas. We review the quality of resulting inquiries with your team.

Does the form replace a technical move assessment?

No. It collects enough information to start a conversation. Your qualified operations team remains responsible for assessing feasibility, site conditions, and the requirements of the move.

Can we advertise both crane work and machinery moving?

Yes, with separate service messaging and suitable intake fields. That helps route each inquiry to the right specialist instead of treating every project as general freight.
