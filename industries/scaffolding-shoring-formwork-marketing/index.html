Can you market scaffolding, shoring and formwork separately?

Yes. Access equipment, temporary support and concrete formwork involve different buyer needs. We give the offers useful pages and campaign messages so contractors can find the right starting point.

What should a project inquiry include?

Ask about the project location, type of work, approximate requirements and required dates. Find out whether the buyer needs rental, supply or additional support. Your technical team should confirm specifications and project suitability before quoting.

How do we explain rental and supply coverage?

Show the areas, products and support your business can provide, along with useful information about delivery and the inquiry process. Keep availability and project requirements subject to your team’s review.

How can we measure which campaigns bring suitable projects?

Record the source, product or system, location and quote outcome where your tools allow. Separate rental, supply and larger project opportunities so the results reflect the type of work your business wants.

What does scaffolding and formwork marketing cost?

It depends on your product lines, coverage, website and campaign needs. We agree on the work and fees before starting. Ad spending and extra software costs are discussed separately.
