Who do you work with in the mining industry?

We can support mining operators, equipment manufacturers, component suppliers, maintenance providers and contractors. We start by understanding your business and whether you want to reach customers, suppliers, partners or other audiences.

How can a mining supplier attract more relevant inquiries?

Explain the equipment, components or services you offer, the applications you support and the regions you cover. Search campaigns and useful product pages can then guide buyers to an inquiry form or sales contact that fits their needs.

What should a mining equipment or service inquiry include?

Start with the equipment or service needed, site location, application and project stage. Depending on the request, your team may also need the equipment make and model, operating conditions or required timing before discussing a quote.

Can you help market to buyers with long purchasing cycles?

Yes. We can help keep the inquiry source, follow-up and proposal stages visible in your customer records. Your team can track technical reviews and procurement discussions as the buyer moves through its own process.

What does marketing for a mining company cost?

The cost depends on the markets, content, website and campaigns involved. We discuss the scope and fees before work begins; advertising spend and any additional software are considered separately.
