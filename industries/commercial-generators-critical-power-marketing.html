05 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before we get started.

Can campaigns exclude residential generator work?

We can focus language, search terms, landing pages, and qualification on commercial applications. Search-term review helps identify and reduce irrelevant inquiries over time.

Do technical guides need to be gated?

Not always. Some information is most useful as an open resource, while an optional download can support a longer sales conversation. We choose based on buyer needs and the content available.

Will you recommend system sizes on the website?

Engineering recommendations should come from your qualified team. Marketing can collect application details and explain the consultation process without presenting an unverified design or sizing recommendation.
