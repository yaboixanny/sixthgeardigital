05 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Common questions.

Can we separate urgent calls from new projects?

Yes. Use a clear phone route for service and a separate form for planned work.

Can the website tell buyers what power system they need?

It can explain your services and collect details. Your qualified team should assess the site and recommend the system.
