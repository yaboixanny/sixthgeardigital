05 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before we get started.

Can we avoid restaurant and domestic repair inquiries?

We can focus campaign and page language on industrial facilities and review search terms for irrelevant demand. Qualification questions further establish the type of facility and project.

What technical claims belong on the website?

Use current, approved statements about your own capabilities, qualifications, and project scope. System design, safety, and compliance assessments should remain with qualified professionals.

Can project videos help a longer buying cycle?

Yes. Approved walkthroughs and project explanations can help stakeholders understand your approach. We connect them to useful next steps rather than presenting unsupported project outcomes.
