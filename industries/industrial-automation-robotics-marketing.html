PLANNING THE BUYER JOURNEY

What makes a useful inquiry in this sector?

The buying decision

Operations, engineering and procurement may evaluate the same integration project for different reasons. Demonstration views alone do not establish a funded or feasible project.

The page and intake

Connect demonstrations to application pages that explain process, supported integration scope and approved project context. Capture current equipment, intended change and project stage for discovery.

The qualification check

Track application fit, discovery, feasibility and proposal milestones. Record why an opportunity pauses so marketing can distinguish timing issues from poor audience fit.

Build this journey with industrial application content and SEO and technical sales follow-up and tracking.

05 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before we get started.

Can you promote our existing robot demonstration videos?

Yes. We pair approved footage with application context and a useful inquiry path. That helps distinguish commercial project interest from general views.

How do you support a long sales cycle?

We plan resources and follow-up around the stages your sales team uses, from initial discovery to feasibility and proposal review. Measurement should account for that longer process.

Do account-based campaigns guarantee access to named buyers?

No. They help focus a campaign on relevant account and role groups where the platform supports it. Reach and results still depend on audience availability, content, budget, and execution.
