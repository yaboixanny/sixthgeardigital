PLANNING THE BUYER JOURNEY

What makes a useful inquiry in this sector?

The buying decision

Equipment sales, short-term rental and repair calls should not share one generic enquiry route. Buyers may know a model or only the load, lift height and operating environment.

The page and intake

Create distinct pages for equipment, rental and service. Use approved capacity and application information; ask for the operating requirement so a qualified representative can assess suitability.

The qualification check

Compare inquiry quality within each business line. Record rental duration, equipment fit or service coverage before treating an enquiry as qualified.

05 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before we get started.

Can each branch have its own search presence?

Yes. Branch pages and local listings should reflect real locations, coverage, and services. We build around those facts rather than creating unsupported location claims.

Can you promote used stock?

Yes, provided inventory information is kept current. We can scope product pages or a stock feed around the systems and update process you use.

Should AGV projects share a forklift rental page?

They can sit within the same website, but the inquiry paths should differ. AGV projects need workflow and site context, while rental buyers often need a quicker equipment discussion.
