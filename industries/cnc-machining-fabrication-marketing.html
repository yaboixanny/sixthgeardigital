05 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before we get started.

Can we qualify for production work rather than prototypes?

Yes. We can make your preferred work clear and ask about quantities, timing, and project type. Qualification should reflect your real commercial thresholds, including any prototype work you do want.

How should proprietary CAD files be handled?

We first agree on your approved file-transfer process and any contractual or security requirements. This public marketing inquiry form does not accept drawings or claim a regulated file-handling capability.

How quickly will campaigns produce RFQs?

Timing depends on demand, competition, your website, and buyer fit. We establish a baseline, launch with suitable tracking, and assess relevant inquiries without promising a fixed number or timeline.
