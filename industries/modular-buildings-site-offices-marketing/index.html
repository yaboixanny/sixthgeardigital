Can you market modular building sales and rentals separately?

Yes. A buyer purchasing a building needs different details from someone arranging a temporary site office. We give those offers clear pages, relevant ads and questions that help your team understand the request.

What details should a modular building inquiry include?

Ask about the intended use, location, approximate size, required date and whether the customer wants to buy or rent. For rentals, include the expected duration. Your team can discuss access, utilities and site requirements before quoting.

Should we create pages for each building type?

Useful pages for site offices, classrooms, welfare units or other products can help buyers compare options. Each page should explain a real offer with its own specifications and applications, rather than repeat the same content under a new title.

How can we see which marketing produces usable projects?

Track purchase and rental requests separately. Record the building type, location, timing, quote stage and final outcome where your tools allow. That gives a clearer picture than combining every call into one lead total.

What does modular building marketing cost?

Costs depend on the building range, delivery areas, campaigns and website work. We agree on the scope and fees before starting, and discuss ad spending or extra tools separately.
