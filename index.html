Different customer needs

A repair call is different from a project quote.

Someone with a broken machine needs help quickly. Someone planning a large project needs more time and detail. Your website should help both find the next step.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with manufacturers and service companies?

Yes. We plan the work around what your customers need. A repair company may need local ads and clear phone contact. A manufacturer may need detailed service pages and a quote form.

How long does SEO take?

SEO takes time. Your website, competition and market affect how quickly things change. We review progress, but do not promise a certain ranking or number of calls.

Can you show which inquiries become jobs?

That depends on your tools and sales records. We can help connect calls and forms with quotes and jobs, and make it clear where information is missing.

How much should we spend on Google Ads?

There is no single budget that suits every company. We look at your services, location, competition and goals. What you pay Google is discussed separately from our fee.
