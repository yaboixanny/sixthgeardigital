Different customer needs

A repair call is different from a project quote.

Someone with a broken machine needs help quickly. Someone planning a large project needs more time and detail. Your website should help both find the next step.

For businesses that serve other businesses
01 / REPAIRS

Repairs and field service

When equipment stops working, people need to know if you can help and how to reach you.

Example A broken hydraulic hose or a forklift that will not start.
What the page needs Your service area, opening hours and an easy-to-find phone number.
What to review Calls you can help with
02 / EQUIPMENT

Equipment sales and rentals

Buyers need to compare options and check whether the equipment fits the job.

Example A forklift rental or an attachment for an excavator.
What the page needs Clear product details and a form that asks about the job, location and dates.
What to review Useful quote requests
03 / PROJECTS

Larger projects and custom work

Larger jobs may involve several people and take months to plan. Buyers need clear details about the work you can handle.

Example A steel building, a new production line or a batch of custom parts.
What the page needs Examples of your work and a simple way to share the first project details.
What to review Projects that fit
