FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work with both custom manufacturers and field service teams?

Yes. The approach reflects the buying situation: service teams often need local visibility and clear phone contact, while custom manufacturers need capability information and quote requests that support a longer technical evaluation.

How long does industrial SEO take to produce qualified RFQs?

SEO is a longer-term investment. Timing depends on your website, competition, technical content, and the markets you serve. We establish a baseline and review progress through relevant search visibility and inquiry quality rather than promise a fixed timeline.

Can you track leads through to closed purchase orders?

That depends on your tracking, CRM, and how your team records sales outcomes. We can scope a measurement approach that connects inquiries with later stages of your pipeline, and identify any integration work needed before launch.

What monthly ad budget is required for industrial Google Ads?

There is no single budget that fits every industrial business. Geography, search demand, competition, margins, and the value of a new customer all matter. We discuss media spend separately from agency work and scope a starting budget around your goals.
