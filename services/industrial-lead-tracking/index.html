06 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before we get started.

Can you work with our current CRM?

We start by reviewing the CRM, phone system, form tools, and available integration options. The scope depends on access, supported fields, permissions, and how your team uses those systems.

Does tracking identify the source of every job?

No. Some journeys span multiple devices, offline conversations, or incomplete records. Reporting should distinguish known sources from unknown or partially attributed outcomes.

Do we need to record every phone call?

Not necessarily. The right setup depends on the information needed and the tools in use. Call recording, where proposed, needs its own assessment of notice, consent, access, and retention requirements.

Can follow-up run automatically?

Some tasks, reminders, and approved messages can be automated where systems and communication permissions support them. Sales still needs to own the opportunity and keep its status accurate.
