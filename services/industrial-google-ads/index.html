01 / COMMON PROBLEMS

Make your ads useful to the people who see them.

The search term, the page and the response all affect whether an ad brings useful work.

01 / CHALLENGE

Some searches bring the wrong calls

The same search can mean different things. We choose terms that match your work and exclude searches that do not.

02 / CHALLENGE

The page does not answer the question

Someone with a broken machine needs a phone number and service hours. An equipment buyer needs product details. Each should reach a page that helps.

03 / CHALLENGE

More calls do not always mean more work

Some calls are spam or requests you cannot handle. Feedback from your team helps us see which ads bring useful inquiries.

03 / DIFFERENT CUSTOMER NEEDS

Different needs call for different ads.

Give people the details they need for the job they have in mind.

Repairs and field service

Make your service area, hours and phone number easy to find. Run ads when your team can answer calls.

  • Equipment type
  • Job location
  • How soon help is needed

Equipment sales and rentals

Show what the equipment does and who it is for. Ask a few questions so your team can check fit and prepare a quote.

  • Equipment needed
  • Delivery location
  • Purchase or rental dates

Fabrication and project work

Explain the work you do and the details needed to quote it. Buyers may need time to compare suppliers.

  • Type of work
  • Quantity or project size
  • Required dates

06 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Common questions.

Is the ad budget included in your fee?

No. We discuss what you pay Google separately from our work. We agree on the budget, pages and tracking before the ads start.

Will every inquiry be a good fit?

No. Clear ads, useful pages and sensible form questions can help, but some inquiries will still be unsuitable. Your team’s feedback helps us improve the ads.

Can you use our existing Google Ads account?

Yes. We can review your account and past results. We arrange access through Google Ads, so you do not need to share your password.

How do you judge the results?

We look at the cost of useful inquiries and whether they lead to quotes or jobs. Clicks and calls help explain what happened, but they do not tell the whole story.
