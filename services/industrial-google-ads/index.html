01 / THE CHALLENGE

Make the search match the commercial opportunity.

Campaign quality depends on targeting, the page a buyer reaches, and what happens when they get in touch.

01 / CHALLENGE

Broad searches blur intent

Repair, equipment, and fabrication terms can attract very different buyers. Campaigns need a clear definition of the work you want and the inquiries you cannot serve.

02 / CHALLENGE

Traffic reaches the wrong page

A buyer searching for emergency repair needs a fast service path. An equipment buyer needs specifications. Sending both to the same generic page creates friction.

03 / CHALLENGE

Conversion counts hide quality

A phone call or submitted form is a starting point. Sales feedback is needed to distinguish a relevant commercial inquiry from spam, unsuitable work, or an existing customer request.

03 / BUYING SITUATIONS

Campaigns shaped around how your buyers buy.

Match the journey to the buyer’s urgency, technical needs, and decision process.

Urgent repairs & field service

Use clear service coverage, availability, and phone contact for buyers dealing with a breakdown. Campaign schedules should reflect when your team can respond.

  • Equipment type
  • Job location
  • Urgency & callback

Equipment sales & rentals

Help buyers evaluate the right machine or rental category. Keep inventory and product claims accurate, and collect the application details needed for a quote.

  • Equipment requirement
  • Delivery location
  • Purchase or rental timing

Fabrication & contractor projects

Support longer evaluations with capability information and structured RFQs. Ask about project scope, quantity or scale, and the intended schedule.

  • Capability or project scope
  • Quantity or project scale
  • Required dates

06 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before we get started.

Is media spend included in your management scope?

Media spend and agency work should be agreed separately. We establish the campaign scope, landing-page needs, tracking work, and proposed advertising budget before launch.

Can Google Ads produce only qualified industrial leads?

No campaign can guarantee that every inquiry will qualify. We use relevant targeting, clear pages, sensible intake questions, and sales feedback to improve fit over time.

Can you work with our existing account?

Yes. A review can start with your existing structure, historical results, and current conversion setup. Any account access is arranged separately through the appropriate platform permissions.

How do you judge campaign performance?

We look at qualified inquiry cost and progression into quotes or won work where reliable data is available. Calls, forms, and clicks remain useful diagnostics, but each needs context.
