01 / COMMON PROBLEMS

Make the next step easy to find.

A useful page answers the buyer’s questions and helps your team respond.

01 / CHALLENGE

One page tries to do too much

A homepage cannot explain every service in detail. Buyers need pages that answer the questions behind their visit.

02 / CHALLENGE

Important details are buried

Long catalogs and unfamiliar terms can make a site hard to use. Clear headings and simple menus help buyers find what matters.

03 / CHALLENGE

Forms ask too much or too little

A short form may leave your team chasing basic details. A long one may put people off. We ask for enough to start a useful conversation.

06 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Common questions.

Can you work with our existing website?

Yes. A few page changes may be enough. We review the site before suggesting a full rebuild.

Can buyers send CAD drawings through a form?

We can discuss that as part of the work. First, we need to agree on how files will be sent, stored and shared. Use the general contact form for basic details, not private drawings.

Will a rebuild affect traffic from search?

It can, so we plan for it. We keep useful content and page addresses where possible. If an address changes, we send visitors from the old page to the new one.

What happens after someone asks for a quote?

We agree on who gets the request, what details they receive and how they follow up. The form should help your team start the next conversation.
