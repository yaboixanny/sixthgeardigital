01 / COMMON PROBLEMS

Keep track of requests after they arrive.

Clear records help your team know who needs a reply and what happens next.

01 / CHALLENGE

Details are spread across different tools

Calls, emails and spreadsheets may each hold part of the story. It can be hard to see which ads or pages led to a job.

02 / CHALLENGE

No one is sure what happens next

One person may think a request is new while another has sent a quote. Shared steps help the team stay on the same page.

03 / CHALLENGE

Quotes are sent and then forgotten

Without a follow-up date or a recorded result, it is easy to lose track of a quote. Reports also miss whether the request became work.

Product and equipment quotes

06 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Common questions.

Can you use our current CRM?

We start by checking your customer management software, phone system and form tools. What we can connect depends on the features they support.

Can you tell where every job came from?

No. People may use different devices, call directly or hear about you offline. Reports should show when the source is unknown.

Do we need to record every call?

No. We choose a setup based on what you need to know. If call recording is part of it, we first agree on consent, access and how long recordings are kept.

Can follow-up happen automatically?

Some reminders and messages can. That depends on your tools and permission to contact the buyer. Your team still needs to respond to questions and keep the records current.
